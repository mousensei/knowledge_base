### Project Overview

**Project Positioning:**

Zhanggui AI Knowledge Base is an enterprise-grade intelligent knowledge management system built on Retrieval-Augmented
Generation (RAG) technology. It aims to provide accurate and intelligent knowledge retrieval and question-answering
services for specialized domains, such as electronic product manuals, repair guides, and technical documentation.

**Core Objectives:**

- Transform unstructured documents (PDF, Markdown) into structured, searchable knowledge.
- Improve retrieval accuracy through multi-channel retrieval strategies.
- Deliver a seamless, real-time conversational experience with streaming responses.
